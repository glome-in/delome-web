Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, what permissions we request, and what you can do about your data.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Delome collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

We only request access to information and device features that are necessary to provide and improve the features of the app. You can control many permissions through your device settings.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Delome, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and profile picture.
  • Photographs: photographs of rooms or other spaces that you choose to capture or upload for design generation.
  • Home-service information: saved address, recipient name, recipient phone number, and other information necessary to arrange a requested service.
  • Location information: location information when you choose to use a feature that requires your location, such as helping identify or provide a service location.
  • Usage information: designs generated, services booked, credits used or spent, posts you like or save, and messages you send to support.
  • Device and technical information: information necessary for the app to communicate with our servers and provide its features, such as network connectivity and basic device-related information.

3. Requested Data and Why We Need It

The following table explains the main categories of information requested by Delome and the reason each category is used.

Data Why We Need It How You Provide It
Name To identify your account and provide requested services. Sign-in or information entered by you.
Email address To identify your account and communicate with you when necessary. Account/sign-in information.
Phone number To support account and home-service functionality when required. Sign-in or information entered by you.

4. App Permissions

Delome may request access to certain device capabilities. We request these permissions only when the associated feature needs them.

Camera Permission

Why we request it: Camera access allows you to take photographs of rooms or spaces directly within the Delome app.

Camera access is used only when you choose a feature that requires taking a photograph. Delome does not use your camera continuously or secretly record through your camera.

Storage / Photos and Media Permission

Why we request it: Storage or photo-library access allows you to select photographs already stored on your device and upload them to Delome.

We may also use local device storage to temporarily cache recently viewed images and information so the application can load faster and provide a better experience when connectivity is poor.

Access to photographs is used for content that you choose to select or upload. We do not use your personal photographs for advertising.

Internet / Network Permission

Why we request it: Internet access is required for Delome to communicate with its servers and provide online features.

This includes account authentication, uploading photographs, generating or retrieving designs, processing service requests, maintaining your credit balance, sending and receiving information, and communicating with support.

Internet access may also be required to retrieve information from third-party services used by the app.

GPS / Location Permission

Why we request it: Location permission may be requested when you use a location-based feature, such as helping identify a relevant service location or using your current location for a home service.

Location access is not required for every feature of Delome. You can deny or disable location permission through your device settings. If you do so, features that depend on your current location may not work correctly.

Where possible, you may manually enter an address instead of providing your current device location.

5. Why We Collect and Use Your Information

We use the information described above for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your Delome account.
  • Generating designs from photographs you submit.
  • Allowing you to select or capture photographs.
  • Arranging requested home services at the appropriate location.
  • Maintaining an accurate credit balance.
  • Processing service bookings.
  • Providing customer and technical support.
  • Saving your preferences and improving app performance.
  • Providing features that require an internet connection.
  • Providing optional location-based functionality.

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not use your room photographs for advertising.

6. Services and Third Parties

Delome uses certain third-party services to provide its functionality.

Google Firebase Authentication

Sign-in and authentication are handled through Google Firebase Authentication. Information necessary to authenticate your account may be processed by Firebase.

Cloudflare R2

Photographs that you choose to upload are stored using Cloudflare R2 storage. The Delome API records the storage location needed to retrieve those files.

India Post Public Directory

When you enter a PIN code, the six-digit PIN code may be sent to India Post's public directory to look up the corresponding city and state.

Your street address, name, and Delome account information are not included in that PIN-code lookup.

7. Information Stored on Your Device

Some information is stored locally on your device to make the app faster and more convenient.

  • Recently viewed lists.
  • Recently viewed images.
  • Cached information required for app performance.
  • Notification preferences.
  • Theme preferences.

Local cached information is cleared when you sign out where applicable.

8. Sharing to the Public Feed

Designs you generate are not automatically published to the public feed.

A design is shared publicly only when you deliberately choose to share it. Once shared, other users may be able to view the shared design and associated content that is made public through the sharing feature.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by Delome against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You can control your information and permissions in several ways.

  • You can deny camera, storage/photo, or location permissions through your device settings.
  • You can edit or remove saved addresses from the app.
  • You can change notification preferences.
  • You can change your theme preferences.
  • You can choose whether to share a generated design to the public feed.
  • You can delete your Delome account from Settings → Account Settings.
  • You can contact us to request information about the personal data associated with your account.

Some features may not function when a required permission is denied or disabled.

11. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Delome account through the account settings available in the application.

When an account is deleted, we remove the account record from our systems, subject to information that we may be required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

12. Children

Delome is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will provide notice in the app before the change takes effect where reasonably required.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to request information about your data, or want to raise a privacy concern, please contact us.
